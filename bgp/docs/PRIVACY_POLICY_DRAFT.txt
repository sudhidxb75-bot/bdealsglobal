Privacy and No Direct Dealing Policy Draft
=========================================

This platform operates as a real estate mediator. Buyer and seller details are not publicly displayed. Property owner contact details, buyer contact details, exact property location, property documents, negotiation limits, and internal transaction notes are kept in the backend and handled only by the authorized platform team.

Public property listings show only limited information such as property type, locality, approximate size, price range, basic highlights, and property code. Detailed information is shared only after verification and platform approval.

Users agree that they will not attempt to bypass the platform after receiving property information, buyer information, seller information, site visit access, or negotiation support through the platform.

The platform may earn income through success commission, service fees, listing fees, builder packages, documentation support charges, or other agreed service charges.
